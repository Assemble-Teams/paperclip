# Advisory Pilot

Use this optional team to run a private advisory engagement with a small, governed AI operating team.

- `ATLAS` coordinates context, priorities, and decision preparation.
- `RAS` independently reviews evidence, completion criteria, and material claims.
- `GROWTH` supports relationship, opportunity, and follow-up work.
- `DELIVERY` supports engagement execution, actions, risks, and closure.

This catalog fixture is intentionally generic. Keep client identities, qualification thresholds, commercial terms, scheduling rules, credentials, and proprietary advisory playbooks in private company configuration, private skills, or approved private workspaces rather than this package.

Human operators retain authority over consequential external communications, commitments, approvals, access changes, and engagement closure.
